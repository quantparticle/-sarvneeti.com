03 / RESOLUTION TIMELINE

Timely support.
Clear follow-up.

The Research Analyst should strive to address investor grievances promptly and within the timeframe required under applicable regulatory rules.

A complaint may require supporting documents or additional information. Please retain records of your complaint, communications and any acknowledgement received.

01

Complaint received

The investor provides a clear description of the concern and relevant supporting information.

02

Review and resolution

Grievances should be addressed without unnecessary delay. The applicable SEBI investor charter describes a 21-day resolution timeframe from receipt.

03

Further escalation

If dissatisfied with the response, investors may use SEBI SCORES or the applicable Online Dispute Resolution mechanism.

04 / REGULATORY ESCALATION

Further help
for investors.

Official regulatory mechanisms are available for eligible complaints and unresolved securities-market disputes.

SEBI COMPLAINT PORTAL

SEBI SCORES

SCORES is SEBI's centralized complaint redressal platform. Investors may lodge and track eligible complaints through the official portal, subject to its applicable rules and procedures.

VISIT SCORES ↗
ONLINE DISPUTE RESOLUTION

SMART ODR

SMART ODR provides an online mechanism for eligible securities-market disputes, including conciliation and arbitration, in accordance with the applicable framework.
