SECTION 01

Our Privacy Commitment

Sarvneeti recognizes the importance of privacy and the responsible handling of personal information.

We aim to be transparent about the information collected through our website and research-related communication channels, the purposes for which that information is used, and the choices available to individuals.

Personal information should be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the relevant purposes disclosed to users.

▤
SECTION 02

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through our website, enquiry forms, complaint forms, research subscriptions or official communication channels.

Depending on the interaction, this may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact or mobile number
  • Research service interests and enquiry details
  • Complaint descriptions and follow-up information
  • Subscription and onboarding details where applicable
  • Records of communications relating to our services

Additional information may be collected where necessary for applicable regulatory, contractual or lawful operational requirements. Such collection should be explained at the relevant point of collection.

◎
SECTION 03

How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used for legitimate and disclosed purposes connected with Sarvneeti's operations.

  • Responding to enquiries and service-related questions
  • Providing subscribed research services and relevant updates
  • Completing applicable onboarding and account-related procedures
  • Handling complaints, support requests and grievance follow-up
  • Maintaining records required for legal or regulatory compliance
  • Improving service quality and communication processes
  • Sending optional promotional communications where permitted by law and supported by the appropriate consent or authority

We do not treat an enquiry as permission to use personal data for unrelated purposes.

✓
SECTION 04

Data Accuracy & Protection

Sarvneeti aims to maintain accurate personal information and take reasonable steps to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of data under its control.

Appropriate safeguards may include restricted access to relevant records, controlled access to service tools, secure account practices and oversight of authorized personnel and service providers.

No electronic storage, communication or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, trading PINs, OTPs, security answers or other secret account credentials through website enquiry or complaint forms.

Where an unauthorized access or data-security incident occurs, applicable legal and regulatory obligations will govern the necessary response.

✉
SECTION 05

Service & Promotional Communication

Sarvneeti may contact individuals regarding enquiries, subscriptions, support requests, regulatory notices, service updates and other relevant operational matters.

These communications may take place through appropriate channels, including email, telephone, SMS or WhatsApp.

Optional promotional communications, newsletters, surveys, new-service announcements or marketing messages will be handled in accordance with applicable consent and telecommunications requirements.

You may request to stop optional promotional communications through the relevant communication channel or our official contact facility.

Opting out of promotional communication may not prevent necessary transactional, service-related or legally required notices.

↗
SECTION 06

Sharing of Information

Personal information may be shared where reasonably necessary for permitted and disclosed purposes or as required by applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, recipients may include:

  • Regulatory or governmental authorities where disclosure is lawfully required
  • Authorized service providers involved in website hosting, communication or data processing
  • Professional service providers where relevant to legitimate operational or legal needs
  • Other recipients where you have appropriately authorized the disclosure

Sharing should be limited to relevant purposes and subject to applicable confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations.

We do not treat this Privacy Policy as unrestricted permission to share personal information for unrelated purposes.

⌁
SECTION 07

Enquiry Forms & Contact Consent

When you submit an enquiry or contact Sarvneeti through an official communication channel, the information you provide may be used to respond to the specific request and undertake relevant follow-up communication.

This may include contact by email, phone or other relevant channels using the contact details supplied.

Submission of an enquiry does not automatically constitute blanket consent for promotional calls, unrelated marketing messages or communications inconsistent with applicable DND and telecommunications requirements.

If a website form is connected to an authorized data-storage service, the submitted information may be processed through that service for the disclosed purpose.

The collection notice and consent presented alongside each form should be read together with this Privacy Policy.

◷
SECTION 08

Data Retention & Record Management

Information may be retained for the period reasonably required to fulfil the relevant purpose for which it was collected, including service delivery, complaint handling, lawful operational requirements and regulatory recordkeeping.

Certain information may need to be retained for periods prescribed by applicable law or regulatory requirements.

Where information is no longer required for an applicable purpose, its deletion, anonymization or continued preservation will be assessed according to applicable obligations.

Requests for deletion or correction will be considered subject to identity verification, legal retention obligations and other applicable limitations.

◇
SECTION 09

Your Choices & Privacy Requests

Individuals may contact Sarvneeti regarding personal information provided through the website or research services.

Depending on applicable law and the relevant circumstances, requests may concern:

  • Access to information about the processing of personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Withdrawal of consent where consent is the relevant lawful basis
  • Deletion of data where permitted and not subject to retention obligations
  • Communication preferences and related privacy concerns

Requests can be initiated through the Sarvneeti Contact section . We may require reasonable verification before acting on requests involving personal information.

The response process will be governed by applicable laws and the nature of the request.

▣
SECTION 10

Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, regulatory resources and external communication platforms.

Where authorized third-party services are used for data storage, form processing, hosting or communication, their role should be assessed under the applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Third-party websites may operate under separate privacy policies and terms. Users should review those documents before providing personal information directly to external services.

If optional analytics, advertising technologies or non-essential cookies are introduced, the applicable notices and choices should be provided before they are used where required.

↻
SECTION 11

Policy Updates & Our Commitment

Sarvneeti may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in services, data processing practices, operational requirements or applicable laws.

Material changes requiring particular notice or consent will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Users are encouraged to review the currently published version of this policy when using the website or research services.

We value the trust of our visitors and clients and aim to maintain responsible, transparent handling of their personal information.
