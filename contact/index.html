SEND YOUR QUESTION ↗
How do I subscribe to a research plan?

Contact our research desk, review the applicable fees, client agreement and disclosures, and complete onboarding before service activation.

Can I contact you before choosing a plan?

Yes. You can select General Research Enquiry in the form to request more information before deciding on a plan.

Are plan prices inclusive of taxes?

Plan prices are displayed before applicable statutory charges. Relevant fees and payment conditions should be confirmed before subscribing.

How are research updates delivered?

Depending on the research plan, communication may include email, WhatsApp or a designated research channel.

Is any return guaranteed?

No. Research Analyst registration does not guarantee any investment performance or outcome. Options trading involves high risk.

Does Sarvneeti manage my trading funds?

No. The research service does not take custody of client funds or execute trades on behalf of clients.
