OptionNeeti is a dedicated index options research experience covering Nifty, Bank Nifty and Sensex. It brings together market observations, potential intraday and BTST setups, the reasoning behind each idea and the factors that could invalidate the research view.

Built for traders who want to examine a research thesis—not blindly follow an alert. OptionNeeti communicates possible entry zones, potential targets, stop-loss context and relevant expiry or volatility risks when applicable.
