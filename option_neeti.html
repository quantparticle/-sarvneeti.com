Which index options are covered?

Nifty and Bank Nifty are primary areas. Sensex and selected indices are covered depending on plan and market conditions.

Will I receive a call every day?

Not necessarily. Each plan specifies the maximum. Some sessions may not produce qualifying setups.

How do I access premium Telegram?

Channel access is shared after the applicable onboarding and plan activation.

What are the risks of BTST options?

Overnight gaps, premium decay and changes in volatility can cause rapid and substantial losses.

Are profits or accuracy guaranteed?

No. SEBI registration or NISM certification does not guarantee performance or returns.

Do you manage trading accounts?

No. Research and recommendations are provided. Clients independently make trading decisions.

Where can I get plan fees?

Contact the research desk for subscription charges, taxes, terms and applicable policies.
