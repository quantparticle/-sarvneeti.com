Frequently asked questions

Know before you subscribe.

Which indices do you cover?

Research primarily focuses on Nifty and Bank Nifty options, with Sensex and other selected index setups depending on the plan and market conditions.

Will I get calls every trading day?

Each plan has a stated maximum number of research setups. Research is conditional on market opportunities; there may be sessions where no qualifying setup is shared.

What is BTST, and what are its risks?

BTST means buy today, sell tomorrow. Holding index options overnight can expose traders to market gaps, changes in implied volatility and time decay. Losses can be rapid or substantial.

How do I receive premium Telegram alerts?

After the advisory desk completes applicable onboarding, disclosures and plan activation, access details are shared through the agreed communication channel.

Do you trade or manage client funds?

No. This service provides research and recommendations, not order execution, portfolio management or custody of client funds. You make all investment and execution decisions yourself.

Is profit or accuracy guaranteed?

No. SEBI registration and NISM certification do not guarantee an analyst's performance or any return. Options are high-risk instruments, and research ideas can fail.

How do I know the plan price?

Contact the advisory desk for current fees, duration, taxes, applicable service terms, disclosures and refund or cancellation policies before deciding to subscribe.
