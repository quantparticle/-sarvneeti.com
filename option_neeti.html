05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you
get started.

Speak with our advisory desk to understand services, fees, risk disclosures and onboarding requirements.

Which index options are covered?

Research focuses on Nifty and Bank Nifty, with Sensex and selected indices included depending on the chosen plan and market conditions.

Will I receive a call every day?

Not necessarily. Each plan states an upper limit on setups, and some sessions may not produce a qualifying opportunity.

How is Telegram access provided?

Access to the applicable advisory channel is shared once the relevant subscription and onboarding process is completed.

What are the risks of BTST options?

Holding options overnight involves gap risk, time decay and changes in implied volatility; option premiums can fall sharply.

Do you guarantee profits or accuracy?

No. SEBI registration or NISM certification does not guarantee performance or any returns, and index options can cause substantial losses.

Do you manage my trading account?

No. This is a research and recommendations service. You independently decide whether to trade and retain control of your account.

How can I find out the plan fees?

Contact our desk to review the current fees, subscription terms, taxes, disclosures and applicable policies before subscribing.
