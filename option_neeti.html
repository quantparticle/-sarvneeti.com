Which index options are covered?

Research primarily covers Nifty and Bank Nifty. Sensex and selected indices may also be covered depending on the plan and market conditions.

How many analyses will I receive?

Coverage depends on the selected plan and market conditions. The number of analyses indicated is not a guarantee that qualifying research setups will arise.

How do I receive research updates?

Communication may include email, WhatsApp or relevant research channels, depending on your selected plan and onboarding.

What are the risks of BTST options?

Overnight gaps, premium decay and changes in volatility can cause rapid and substantial losses.

Are profits or accuracy guaranteed?

No. SEBI registration or NISM certification does not guarantee performance or returns.

Do you manage trading accounts?

No. Research and recommendations are provided. Clients independently make their trading and execution decisions.

Are taxes included in plan prices?

The displayed prices exclude applicable statutory charges. Full fees, payment terms and refund conditions are shared before onboarding.

How do I subscribe?

Select a research plan and contact our desk. The applicable disclosures, agreement and payment process will be explained before your subscription is activated.
