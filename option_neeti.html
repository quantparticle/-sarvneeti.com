05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answers,
without the noise.

Have more questions about research coverage, subscription terms or advisory onboarding? Contact the desk directly.

Which index options do you cover?

Nifty and Bank Nifty are key research markets. Sensex and selected indices may be covered depending on your plan and prevailing market conditions.

Will I get a trade call every day?

No fixed number of setups is promised. Plan limits describe the maximum possible coverage, and a trading day may have no qualifying research opportunity.

How do I receive Telegram research updates?

Following applicable onboarding and subscription activation, the research desk shares access to the communication channel corresponding to your plan.

Does BTST carry overnight risk?

Yes. Holding options overnight adds gap risk, time decay and volatility changes. Premiums can fall sharply, and the entire option premium may be lost.

Are profits or accuracy guaranteed?

No. SEBI registration and NISM certification do not guarantee performance or returns. All securities-market investments carry risk; options are particularly risky.

Does the advisory execute trades for me?

No. Research recommendations do not include custody of funds or account access. The subscriber decides whether, when and how much to trade.

How can I check plan pricing?

Contact the advisory desk to receive current subscription fees, duration, applicable taxes and policies before completing onboarding.
