05 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you
get started.

Speak with our research desk to understand services, fees, disclosures, risk considerations and onboarding requirements.

Which index options are covered?

Research primarily covers Nifty and Bank Nifty. Sensex and selected indices are available depending on the plan and market conditions.

Will I receive a call every day?

Not necessarily. Each plan states the maximum number of setups. Some trading sessions may not produce a qualifying opportunity.

How do I access the premium Telegram channel?

Channel details are shared after the applicable onboarding and plan activation.

What are the risks of BTST options?

Overnight positions involve gap risk, premium decay and changes in implied volatility. Losses may be rapid and substantial.

Are profits or accuracy guaranteed?

No. SEBI registration or NISM certification does not guarantee performance or returns. Options involve substantial market risk.

Do you manage my trading account?

No. OptionNeeti provides research and recommendations. Clients independently make trading and execution decisions.

Where can I get the plan fees?

Contact the research desk for subscription charges, taxes, terms, disclosures and applicable policies.
