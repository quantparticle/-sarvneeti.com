04 / WHY OPTIONNEETI

The idea behind
OptionNeeti.

OptionNeeti is built around one principle: an options research update should explain the why, not only the where. Index direction, market structure, setup levels and risks belong together in a usable research explanation.

That is why OptionNeeti also highlights invalidation, expiry effects and premium behaviour. Options are high-risk products, and even researched setups may lose money. There are no guaranteed outcomes.

05 / ABOUT OPTIONNEETI

Your OptionNeeti
questions, answered.

Get to know the OptionNeeti product, the indices it follows, how research is shared, what each plan covers and the risks of index options.

What is OptionNeeti?

OptionNeeti is an index-options research experience focused on explaining potential setups, key market levels, thesis and risks. It covers Nifty, Bank Nifty and, where relevant, Sensex options. It is research, not an order-execution or portfolio-management platform.

Which indices does OptionNeeti research?

OptionNeeti focuses primarily on Nifty and Bank Nifty. Sensex and selected indices may also be included depending on the plan and current market conditions.

How often does OptionNeeti share setups?

The frequency depends on your OptionNeeti plan and whether suitable market conditions occur. A quoted maximum is not a promise that a qualifying setup will appear every day.

Where will I get OptionNeeti updates?

OptionNeeti research may be sent through email, WhatsApp or the applicable research channel, depending on your plan and completed onboarding.

Does OptionNeeti explain BTST risk?

Yes. OptionNeeti BTST research considers overnight gap moves, time decay and volatility changes. These risks can still result in substantial losses.

Does OptionNeeti guarantee option profits?

No. OptionNeeti does not guarantee profits, accuracy or specific outcomes. SEBI registration and NISM certification do not guarantee returns.

Will OptionNeeti trade in my account?

No. OptionNeeti provides research. Your account, order placement, position sizing and execution decisions remain under your control.

Are OptionNeeti plan prices inclusive of tax?

Displayed OptionNeeti prices exclude applicable statutory charges. Check the agreement for final fees, payment schedule and refund conditions.

How do I join OptionNeeti?

Choose an OptionNeeti plan and contact the research desk. Review disclosures, the client agreement, fees and onboarding before activating your research access.
